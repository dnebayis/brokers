# The Desk: internal audit, 2026-10-02

Scope: `src/DeskEngine.sol`, `src/DeskDepositRouter.sol`, `src/DeskAccount.sol`, `src/DeskNFT.sol`,
`src/CoatBonusPool.sol`, `src/DeskRenderer.sol`, and the keeper (`keeper/desk_keeper.py`).
Method: line-by-line review of every function against the promises in `SPEC.md`, Slither 0.11.6
(no findings above informational), and a new test file `test/DeskAudit.t.sol` (unit, fuzz and
invariant tests) plus router audit tests. Every fix below has a test that fails on the old code.

## Findings and fixes

| # | Severity | Finding | Fix | Test |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | High | **The pilot cap could be walked around with stock.** `sellStock` sold any amount in the wallet, and the keeper sold the wallet balance. Shares the owner sent in directly were sold, booked as investable USDG and reinvested, so a $1,000 Desk could invest any amount. | `sellStock` reverts `NotHeld` above `heldQty`; the keeper trades only `min(heldQty, wallet)`. | `test_audit_stockPutInDirectly_isNeverSold`, `invariant_engineNeverSellsMoreThanItBought` (both fail on the old code) |
| 2 | Medium | **Partial fills could strand Desk money in the engine.** An exact-in swap that ran out of range took less than the input; if the fill still cleared the 5% floor, the unspent USDG stayed in the engine. Same for the router's ETH leg (WETH stuck in the router). | The callback must pay exactly the input, once; the swap reverts `PartialFill` otherwise. | `test_audit_partialFillReverts_nothingStranded`, `test_audit_partialFillReverts_noWethLeftBehind` |
| 3 | Medium | **Output was taken from the pool's report.** A misconfigured or hostile pool could report a fill it never delivered. | Output is measured on the recipient's balance. A pool can neither be paid twice nor take more than the input. | `test_audit_poolThatLiesAboutTheFill_reverts` (engine and router), `test_audit_poolCannotTakeMoreThanTheInput`, `test_audit_poolCannotBePaidTwice` |
| 4 | Medium | **One unroutable basket name blocked every first buy.** `buyBasket` reverted on any basket name without a v3 USDG pool (the 7 Rialto names), so a new Desk would never be invested if the live basket held one. | Unrouted names are skipped and the rest scaled up to the whole; the keeper uses the same rule. | `test_audit_unroutedBasketName_isSkipped` |
| 5 | Medium | **A stale or removed feed locked a Desk.** Valuing a withdrawal used the strict swap guard, so after a stock feed went stale or was removed, deposits, buys, sells and the tokenURI's live field all reverted for any Desk that had taken that stock out. | Withdrawals are valued leniently (a stale price counts, no feed frees nothing); swaps stay strict. | `test_audit_brokenFeedNeverLocksADesk` |
| 6 | Low | **The fee conversion had no Chainlink floor**, only the keeper's `minEthOut`. | `flushFees` also requires the Chainlink ETH/USD floor (`setEthUsdFeed`, same feed as the Booster). | `test_audit_feeFlushHasAChainlinkFloor` |
| 7 | Low | **Mint COAT could be swept.** The spec promises 100% of mint COAT to active Brokers, but `sweep` let the owner take unallocated COAT. | COAT is never sweepable; it leaves only through posted rounds. | `test_sweep_neverTouchesCoat` |
| 8 | Info | Unchecked `uint128` casts in the book. | SafeCast. | full suite |

## Invariants (256 runs x 500 calls, two Desks, every user, keeper and market action)

- the engine holds only its own USDG fees: no stock, no WETH, no ETH between transactions;
- a Desk's principal never exceeds the pilot cap;
- the engine never invests USDG that is not in the wallet;
- the engine never sells more shares than it bought;
- a Desk nobody touches never changes.

## Trust assumptions that remain (by design)

- **Keeper.** It decides when to trade, within hard limits: current basket names only, Chainlink
  floor on every fill, 0.5% fee, no destination choice. It could still trade more often than
  needed (fees and up to the slippage floor per trade). The owner can pause their Desk at any time.
- **Owner levers.** Pools, feeds, fee (max 1%), cap and split are settable by the deployer. A bad
  pool is now bounded by findings 2 and 3 and the Chainlink floor.
- **Bonus rounds.** The poster computes the active set off chain; every round is reproducible from
  public Booster events.
- **Selling a Desk.** As with every ERC-6551 wallet, a seller can empty the wallet before a sale
  settles. Buyers should check the wallet at settlement; the account's `state` changes on every
  owner action, which marketplaces can pin.
